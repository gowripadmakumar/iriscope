IRISCOPE processes submitted eye images in memory for experimental visual-texture analysis. Images are not saved, not used for identification or authentication, and this is not a medical diagnostic tool. IRIS MATCH scores are fictional and based only on visual texture — they say nothing real about compatibility, personality, or health.
